Quillbox Plans and Usage Limits

Overview of Plans
Quillbox is an online workspace for writing, organizing, and sharing notes and documents. It is offered in three plans: Free, Pro, and Team. Every new account starts on the Free plan, and no payment details are required to sign up. You can upgrade to Pro or Team at any time from Settings > Billing, and the new plan's features become available immediately after payment is confirmed. Pro is designed for individuals who need more storage and history, while Team adds shared administration for organizations.

Free Plan
The Free plan costs nothing and has no time limit, so you can keep using it indefinitely. It includes up to 3 projects, 1 GB of total file storage, and version history for the last 7 days. Free workspaces can have at most 2 members, including the owner. API access on the Free plan is limited to 500 requests per day. When you reach the 1 GB storage limit, new uploads are blocked, but all existing files remain viewable, editable, and downloadable. Support for Free users is provided through the help center and community forum only.

Pro Plan
The Pro plan costs $12 per user per month when billed monthly, or $120 per user per year when billed annually, which is equivalent to two months free. Pro includes unlimited projects, 50 GB of storage per user, and version history for the last 90 days. Pro workspaces can have up to 10 members. The API limit is 10,000 requests per day per workspace. Pro users can contact email support, and the target first response time is 1 business day.

Team Plan
The Team plan costs $20 per user per month when billed monthly, or $200 per user per year when billed annually. Team includes everything in Pro, plus 100 GB of storage per user, unlimited version history, and unlimited workspace members. The API limit is 50,000 requests per day per workspace. Team adds single sign-on (SSO), role-based permissions, and an audit log that keeps workspace activity records for 1 year. Team customers receive priority email and chat support with a target first response time of 4 business hours.

What Happens When You Exceed a Limit
Quillbox never deletes your documents or files because you exceed a plan limit. If you go over the daily API limit, additional requests receive an HTTP 429 "Too Many Requests" response until the limit resets at 00:00 UTC. Reaching a limit is different from being over it. On the Free plan, reaching the 1 GB storage limit only blocks new uploads, as described above. A workspace can be over its storage, project, or member limit only after moving from a paid plan to Free, and in that case the entire workspace becomes read-only: files can still be viewed, downloaded, and deleted, but nothing can be created, edited, or uploaded until usage is reduced to within the limits or the plan is upgraded. Version history older than the current plan's window is hidden for 30 days and then permanently deleted; upgrading within those 30 days makes it visible again. Details about how plan changes are billed are described in the Billing and Subscriptions document.
